How the Current ratios work

Every complete group of 5 eliminations earns 5 crunches, and every complete group of 5 assists earns 5 lunges. For example, 13 eliminations earn 10 crunches and 12 assists earn 10 lunges.

Six deaths earn 12 jumping jacks. One player card earns a 60-second plank, capped at one card per game.
